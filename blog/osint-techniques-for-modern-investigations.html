OSINT Techniques for Modern Investigations

March 2024 • Guide

Open source intelligence (OSINT) draws on publicly available data to uncover relationships and trace activity. Simple workflows such as pivoting from usernames to social profiles or mapping domain infrastructure can quickly reveal a broader picture of an investigation.

This post highlights a repeatable approach: start with a single indicator, enrich it with search engines and public databases, and document findings as you go. Automating lookups with scripts or dedicated OSINT platforms can further speed up the process while maintaining accuracy.
