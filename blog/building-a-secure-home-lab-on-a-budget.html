Building a Secure Home Lab on a Budget

May 2022 • How‑to

A personal lab makes it possible to safely analyse malware and test defensive tools. Commodity hardware paired with free virtualization software provides isolated networks where experiments cannot leak onto the internet.

Using snapshots to reset machines and segregating them behind a dedicated firewall appliance keeps the environment clean while avoiding expensive commercial solutions.
