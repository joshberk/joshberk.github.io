Reverse Engineering a Botnet Sample

November 2023 • Research Note

The sample began as an unfamiliar executable captured in a honeypot. By inspecting strings and running it in a controlled sandbox, the embedded command-and-control domains and encryption routine quickly came into focus.

Stepping through the code with a debugger revealed a simple state machine that polled its servers for tasks. Understanding this structure allowed the botnet's behaviour to be replicated in a lab and its infrastructure to be taken down.
